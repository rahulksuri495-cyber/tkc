What a celebrity booking agency actually does

A celebrity booking agency in India is the licensed intermediary between brands and artists. The agency owns the artist relationship at the manager level, knows current fee bands, blackout dates and rider requirements, and underwrites the booking commercially. For brands, this removes the friction of cold-outreach, fee inflation by middlemen, and the risk of last-minute artist no-shows. Browse the TKC roster.

How to book a Bollywood celebrity — step by step

Step 1: Share your brief (date, city, event type, audience size, budget). Step 2: The agency returns a shortlist with current fees and availability within 48 hours. Step 3: You confirm an artist; the agency issues a contract and collects 50–100% advance. Step 4: Travel, hospitality and rider are coordinated. Step 5: Artist executes the appearance — typically 60–90 minutes including red carpet, performance/speech and meet-and-greet.

2026 Bollywood fee bands

Top-tier actors (Shah Rukh Khan, Salman Khan, Akshay Kumar): ₹2.5–4 Cr+. A-list (Ranveer Singh, Hrithik Roshan, Deepika Padukone): ₹1.5–3 Cr. Top singers (Arijit Singh, AR Rahman, Diljit Dosanjh): ₹1.2–3.5 Cr per show. Comedians (Kapil Sharma, Zakir Khan): ₹40–90 Lakhs. TV faces and rising stars: ₹15–60 Lakhs. See per-artist fee pages.

Booking by event type

Wedding sangeet: typically a singer or comedian, 90-minute set, ₹40 Lakhs–₹3 Cr. Corporate dealer meet: actor brand integration + 45-minute appearance, ₹1–3 Cr. Product launch with media: actor face-time + Q&A, ₹1.5–3.5 Cr. Public concert with sponsor: singer with full band, ₹2–5 Cr including production. Influencer activations and reel campaigns are a separate vertical.

Why work with an agency vs going direct

Direct outreach to a Bollywood manager rarely works for first-time brands — managers prioritize trusted agencies who underwrite payment and execution. Agencies also know the artist's true current fee (often 30–50% lower than what individual brokers quote), have pre-negotiated paperwork, and carry contingency talent in case of last-minute conflicts.

How to choose the right agency

Look for: (1) a multi-year track record with named brand clients, (2) transparent written fee breakdowns, (3) artist relationships at the management level (not just sub-broker contacts), (4) ability to handle contracts, travel and execution in-house, (5) press coverage and visible artist associations.
