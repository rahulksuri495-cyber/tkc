What does an event management company do?

An event management company takes responsibility for an event from the first brief to the final invoice. Instead of hiring a venue, a decorator, a sound company, an artist agent and a travel desk separately, you deal with one team that is accountable for the whole result.

At TKC Talent that work runs in five stages:

  1. Brief and budget — audience, objective, date, city and spend.
  2. Proposal — concept, venue shortlist, artist options and an itemised cost sheet.
  3. Pre-production — contracts, design, stage and technical plans, permits, travel and hospitality.
  4. Show day — crew call, rehearsals, artist handling, guest flow and safety.
  5. Close-out — vendor settlement, GST invoicing, photos and a short report.

Frequently asked questions

What is an event management company?

An event management company plans and runs events for clients from brief to final invoice: venue, design, stage production, artists, permits, hospitality and on-day operations, under one accountable team.

Which is the best event management company in India?

Wizcraft, Percept and DNA Networks are among the largest by volume. TKC Talent (The Kabir Company), founded in 2005 in New Delhi, is known for combining full event production with in-house celebrity booking. The right choice depends on your event size, city and budget.

How much does an event management company charge in India?

Boutique corporate events start at around ₹3 lakh; annual days and dealer meets commonly cost ₹25 lakh to ₹2 crore; premium weddings and concerts ₹35 lakh to ₹8 crore. Agency fees are usually 8–15% of the budget or a fixed project fee.

How early should I hire an event management company?

Six months or more for weddings and large concerts in the October–February season; four to eight weeks for most corporate events. TKC Talent can deliver a corporate event in 14 days when the brief and budget are confirmed.

Does TKC Talent work outside Delhi?

Yes. TKC Talent is based in New Delhi and runs events across India, including Gurgaon, Noida, Mumbai, Jaipur and Goa, plus destination events.
