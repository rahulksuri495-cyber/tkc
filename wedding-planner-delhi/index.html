Venue

Décor & design

Popular wedding venues in Delhi NCR

  • Five-star hotels: ITC Maurya, The Leela Palace, Taj Mansingh, Roseate House, Oberoi Gurgaon, Leela Ambience.
  • Farmhouses: Chattarpur, Westend Greens and Sultanpur farm venues.
  • Resorts near Delhi: Gurgaon, Sohna and Manesar properties for weekend weddings.

How wedding planning is priced

We quote each wedding individually after understanding guest count, number of functions, venue and entertainment. Planning fees are usually a percentage of the budget (commonly 8–15%) or a fixed fee. Luxury multi-day weddings we manage typically range from ₹35 lakh to ₹8 crore all-in; celebrity performers are quoted separately. For artist fees, see celebrity charges and singer fees 2026.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding planner in Delhi cost?

Planners usually charge 8–15% of the wedding budget or a fixed fee. TKC Talent quotes each wedding individually; luxury multi-day weddings we manage typically total ₹35 lakh to ₹8 crore including venue, décor and entertainment.

When should I book a wedding planner in Delhi?

Six months or more ahead for October–February dates, when venues, décor teams and artists are in highest demand.

Can the wedding planner book a celebrity for the sangeet?

Yes. Singers, actors, comedians and DJs are booked through our in-house team. See book a celebrity for your wedding.

Do you plan weddings in Gurgaon and Noida?

Yes, across Delhi NCR, including Gurgaon, Noida and farmhouse venues around Delhi.

Do you handle destination weddings?

Yes. We plan destination weddings in Jaipur, Goa and other locations with the same team.
