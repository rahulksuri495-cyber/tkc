02 · About

About Alia Bhatt — Bollywood's Awarded Young Star

Alia Bhatt debuted with Student of the Year in 2012 and rapidly built a body of work that spans Highway, 2 States, Udta Punjab, Raazi, Gully Boy, Gangubai Kathiawadi and Rocky Aur Rani Kii Prem Kahaani — collecting six Filmfare Awards and a National Film Award for Best Actress along the way.

She made her Hollywood debut alongside Gal Gadot in Netflix's Heart of Stone (2023) and was appointed Christian Dior's first Indian global ambassador the same year — a pairing that cemented her position as India's most internationally credible young actress.

As a brand ambassador, Alia Bhatt represents L'Oréal Paris, Dior, Maybelline, Jio, Make My Trip, Nykaa and many more — a portfolio that signals trust to luxury, beauty, fashion and lifestyle brands alike.
