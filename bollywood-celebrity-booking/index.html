06FAQ

Bollywood Actor Booking — Fees, process & availability.

How much does it cost to book a Bollywood actor for an event in India?
Bollywood actor fees in India range from ₹20 Lakhs for character and emerging actors to ₹4 Crore+ for A-list superstars like Shah Rukh Khan or Salman Khan. The fee depends on the artist, event type, appearance duration and location. TKC Talent provides a fully itemised quote within 30 minutes.
Which Bollywood actors are available for weddings?
Most Bollywood actors are available for luxury weddings and sangeet nights under signed confidentiality agreements. Popular wedding bookings include Shah Rukh Khan, Salman Khan, Hrithik Roshan, Ranveer Singh, Shahid Kapoor, Varun Dhawan and Kartik Aaryan.
How far in advance should I book a Bollywood actor?
For A-list Bollywood actors, we recommend 90–120 days minimum. During peak wedding season (October to February), 6 months advance planning is strongly advised. Mid-tier actors can sometimes be confirmed within 30–45 days.
What does a Bollywood actor booking include?
Availability confirmation, commercial negotiation, legal performance contract, artist rider management, GST-compliant invoicing, business class travel, 5-star hospitality, personal security coordination, green room setup and a dedicated on-ground TKC coordinator.
Can I book a Bollywood actor for a corporate event?
Yes. Corporate bookings — annual days, product launches, leadership conclaves, brand activations — are one of TKC Talent's most active categories. Akshay Kumar, Ajay Devgn, Ranveer Singh and Kartik Aaryan are particularly popular.
