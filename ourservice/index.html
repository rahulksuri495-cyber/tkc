TKC Talent Services — Frequently Asked Questions

What services does TKC Talent offer?

TKC Talent offers seven core services: luxury wedding celebrity booking, corporate event management, concert & festival production, celebrity brand launches, movie promotion event management, government & institutional events, and trade fair, exhibition & fashion show management — all delivered through a single-window model by The Kabir Company.

Can TKC Talent manage both celebrity booking and full event production?

Yes. TKC Talent manages the full event stack — celebrity booking, travel & hospitality, legal contracts, stage production, sound, lighting, security, green room and on-ground coordination — through one team and one invoice.

Which corporate clients has TKC Talent worked with?

Our corporate roster includes DLF, DS Group, BPTP, Omaxe, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, ICICI Bank, SBI and the Indian Armed Forces, alongside nine state governments and central government ministries including the Ministry of Textiles.

Does TKC Talent manage events outside Delhi?

Yes. TKC Talent operates pan-India — Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, Mumbai, Jaipur, Udaipur, Goa and internationally including Dubai. Destination wedding and international event coverage spans Southeast Asia and Europe.

What makes TKC Talent different from other celebrity booking agencies?

TKC Talent is not an aggregator. We are a 20+ year full-stack event and artist management company with ISO 9001:2015 certification, EEMA Premier Membership and a government-endorsed track record. We manage every step from brief to bow.

Which cities does TKC Talent serve for celebrity bookings?

We deliver celebrity bookings across India with dedicated location pages for Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Mumbai, Jaipur and Goa — plus Udaipur, Faridabad, Dubai and international destination venues on request.
