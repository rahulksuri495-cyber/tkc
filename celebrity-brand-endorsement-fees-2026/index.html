Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a Bollywood celebrity charge for a brand endorsement in 2026?

A-list Bollywood ambassadors (Shah Rukh Khan, Salman Khan, Deepika Padukone) command ₹5–12 Cr per year for a multi-year endorsement. Rising A-list (Kartik Aaryan, Kiara Advani, Rashmika Mandanna) sit at ₹2.5–5 Cr per year. Digital and music icons range ₹60 L–3.5 Cr.

What's the difference between an endorsement fee and an event fee?

An endorsement fee is an annual retainer covering brand exclusivity, shoot days, digital posts and film usage across the contract window. An event fee is a one-time appearance charge. Endorsement is usually 3–5× a single event fee, and comes with tighter usage rights.

Is Alia Bhatt's brand ambassador fee negotiable?

Alia Bhatt's 2026 endorsement fee is ₹6–8 Cr per year. Movement of 10–20% is possible on multi-year deals, category exclusivity, and cross-brand campaigns.

How is a celebrity brand endorsement contract structured?

A standard 2026 endorsement contract runs 12–36 months and specifies: annual fee, shoot days included, digital-post commitments, territory (India / SAARC / global), category exclusivity, morality clauses and post-term takedowns. TKC papers all four commercial and legal layers in-house.

Which Indian celebrity has the most brand endorsements in 2026?

Virat Kohli, Shah Rukh Khan, Deepika Padukone, Alia Bhatt and Ranveer Singh carry the largest 2026 endorsement rosters in India, each with 15–25 active brand deals.

How long does it take to sign a celebrity ambassador?

From brief to signature is typically 6–12 weeks: 1 week shortlist, 2–3 weeks commercial negotiation, 3–5 weeks legal / IP, 1 week shoot planning.

Does TKC Talent handle regional and OTT celebrity endorsements too?

Yes — TKC brokers endorsements across Bollywood, Punjabi, Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam and OTT-native talent, including creators and influencers. Regional A-list endorsement fees typically run 40–70% of Bollywood equivalents.

Are ASCI and deepfake disclosures included in TKC contracts?

Yes. Every 2026 endorsement contract TKC signs includes ASCI-compliant disclosure language, deepfake protections, morality clauses and pre-approved caption templates for social posts.
