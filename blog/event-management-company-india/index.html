How to choose the best event management company

  • Relevant work: ask for two recent events matching your format and audience size.
  • Local capability: confirm venue, vendor, permission and crew strength in your city.
  • Transparent costs: compare agency fees, production markups and contingency line by line.
  • Accountability: identify the event director responsible from planning through show close.
  • Risk planning: review weather, artist, power, medical and cancellation contingencies.
  • Specialist fit: choose corporate, wedding, exhibition or concert experience relevant to the brief.

What does an event management company actually do?

An event management company in India owns the end-to-end execution of a live experience: creative concept, venue scouting, production design (stage, sound, lights, LED, AV), talent and celebrity bookings, vendor management, hospitality, guest logistics, statutory permissions, on-ground crew and post-event reporting. The right partner removes 40+ vendor relationships from your team and replaces them with a single accountable point of contact. Our service framework covers all seven verticals.

Who needs one — and when

Brands need an event company when an in-house team would either lose a quarter of productivity or simply lack the supplier network. Typical triggers: an annual sales offsite over 250 pax, a product launch with media, a dealer meet across multiple cities, a celebrity-led brand activation, a destination wedding, an exhibition booth at India Expo Mart, or a public concert. A good rule: if your event has more than three production verticals (stage + talent + hospitality, for example), outsource it.

Pricing — what an event actually costs in India

Indian event budgets follow predictable bands. A 300-pax corporate offsite at a 5-star Delhi NCR hotel typically runs ₹35–60 Lakhs all-in. A 1,000-pax dealer meet with a Bollywood singer headliner: ₹1.2–2.5 Cr. A celebrity wedding sangeet with two A-list artists: ₹3–8 Cr. The event company's margin is usually 8–15% on managed spend, plus production markups disclosed up-front. Beware agencies that hide markups — see our transparent fee approach.

City coverage that matters for Indian brands

Delhi NCR (Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, Greater Noida) remains India's largest corporate event market by volume. Mumbai owns Bollywood-led activations and BFSI events. Bengaluru runs tech and SaaS launches. Goa and Udaipur lead destination MICE. A pan-India event partner like TKC operates owned crew in Delhi, Mumbai, Gurgaon, Noida, Jaipur and Goa.

12 questions to ask before you sign

1) How many events of my scale did you execute in 2025? 2) Can I speak to two reference clients? 3) Who is my dedicated event director? 4) What is your insurance cover? 5) How do you handle artist no-shows? 6) Is your fee a percentage of spend or a fixed retainer? 7) What is the payment schedule? 8) Which vendors are owned vs subcontracted? 9) What is your contingency plan for weather/venue cancellation? 10) Do you provide a daily WIP report? 11) Who owns the post-event content? 12) What is your cancellation policy?
