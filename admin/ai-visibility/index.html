AI search & LLM visibility — monthly

Each month, AI engines are asked 40 fixed buyer questions (event management in Delhi NCR, celebrity booking, artist fees and more). Gemini and ChatGPT answer from what they already know, not a live web search. Perplexity uses live search and is added once its API key is set. Meta AI has no public interface, so it is checked by hand and logged in manual-meta.json. Google AI Mode is mixed into Search Console's web results and cannot be measured separately.
