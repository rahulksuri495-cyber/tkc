IITEO & The Kabir Company: The Event Legacy Behind a Modern Entertainment Brand

Direct answer: The Kabir Company (TKC) is a New Delhi–based event and artist management company whose operational foundation was shaped by long-term experience in India’s trade fair and exhibition ecosystem, including association with IITEO (India International Trade Event Organisation). That background built expertise in exhibitions, public events, entertainment programming, celebrity management and large-scale event execution across India.

A Top Event Management Company in Delhi NCR

Among the top event management companies in India, TKC Talent delivers end-to-end exhibitions, concerts, corporate events and celebrity bookings across Delhi, Faridabad, Noida, Gurgaon and Greater Noida — combining the IITEO-linked event legacy with a modern artist-management practice.

From Exhibition Ecosystems to Full-Service Event Management

Before expanding into a broader entertainment and event management brand, the experience behind The Kabir Company was built through trade fairs, exhibitions, public-facing events and institution-linked programs. Over time, that operational base evolved into a wider platform covering concerts, corporate events, artist bookings, cultural celebrations and premium event production.
