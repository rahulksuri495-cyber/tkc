What an artist booking agency does

An artist booking agency in India represents performers across genres — Bollywood playback singers, Punjabi pop stars, Sufi vocalists, indie rock bands, electronic DJs and stand-up comedians. The agency holds availability calendars, negotiates fees, issues contracts, manages travel and rider, and ensures the artist takes stage on time. Browse singers, DJs or comedians.

Singers — who's hot in 2026

Top playback: Arijit Singh (₹2.5–3.5 Cr), Shreya Ghoshal (₹80 L–1.5 Cr), Sonu Nigam (₹70 L–1.2 Cr). Punjabi headliners: Diljit Dosanjh (₹1.5–3 Cr), Guru Randhawa (₹60–90 L), B Praak (₹50–80 L). Sufi: Kanika Kapoor (₹30–50 L). Indie/Lo-fi: rising names from ₹2–10 L. See , Diljit Dosanjh, Shreya Ghoshal.

DJs and electronic acts

Celebrity DJs are increasingly booked for weddings and brand activations. International acts (Marshmello, Alan Walker) command ₹2–8 Cr including production. Top Indian DJs: ₹15–50 L. Wedding DJs with sound + lights: ₹3–15 L. Always book through an agency — DJ riders are detailed (specific decks, monitor specs, hospitality) and a missed item kills the set.

Comedians for corporate and private events

Stand-up has become the highest-conversion category for brand events post-2023. Top tier: Kapil Sharma (₹80–90 L), Zakir Khan (₹50–70 L), Vir Das (₹40–60 L). Mid: Anubhav Singh Bassi, Munawar Faruqui, Samay Raina (₹20–40 L). Clean-comic specialists for corporate audiences are a specific shortlist — TKC pre-vets material for brand fit.

How to brief an artist booking agency

Send: (1) event date and city, (2) audience size and profile, (3) budget range, (4) event type (wedding/corporate/concert), (5) desired set length, (6) any genre preferences or no-go names. A complete brief gets a shortlist back in 24 hours. Vague briefs ("a famous singer for a wedding") get vague quotes.

Logistics — what the agency owns

Travel (flights, ground transfer, accommodation), hospitality (artist + entourage, green room, F&B), rider (specific equipment, monitors, mics), security, on-stage flow, photo/video rights and post-event payment release. A good artist booking agency in India removes 100% of the artist-side coordination from the brand team.

How early should I book an artist?

Top-tier artists need 60–120 days of lead time. Mid-tier artists can confirm in 14–30 days. Last-minute (7-day) bookings are possible but limit the roster.

Do you handle artist travel and hospitality?

Yes — flights, ground transfer, accommodation, green room setup, F&B and security are fully managed by the agency, included in the quoted fee.

What's the difference between celebrity and artist booking?

Celebrity booking refers to brand appearances, red-carpet and brand-integration roles. Artist booking refers to live performance — singing, DJ sets or stand-up. TKC handles both verticals.
