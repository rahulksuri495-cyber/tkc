About the Artist

Why Hanumankind owns the stage.

Hanumankind — born Sooraj Cherukat in Malappuram, Kerala and raised between Houston, Texas and Bengaluru — is India's biggest global hip-hop export. His 2024 single Big Dawgs, produced by long-time collaborator Kalmi, became one of the most-streamed rap tracks on the planet that year, charted across the Billboard Global 200, the UK Singles Chart and the Spotify Global Top 50, and earned him a 2025 Coachella main-stage slot.

His live show pairs a razor-sharp bilingual flow with a tightly drilled DJ and crew, a high-energy stadium-grade visual package and the kind of crowd-control that consistently turns festival lawns, college amphitheatres and corporate arenas into single-mind moshpits of 5,000 to 25,000.

TKC Talent has handled multi-star deployments at the scale of Pushpa 2, Singham 3 and Toxic launches, real estate flagship activations for M3M, BPTP and Omaxe, and destination productions across Udaipur, Jaisalmer and Goa. The same operational discipline applies to every Hanumankind booking — production, security, hospitality and a dedicated on-ground TKC coordinator from four hours before arrival until artist departure.
