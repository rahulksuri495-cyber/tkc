How much does corporate event management cost in India?

₹5–25 L for a 200–500 pax town hall, ₹25 L–1 Cr for annual days with a celebrity headliner, and ₹1–4 Cr+ for flagship product launches and dealer meets featuring A-list Bollywood talent.
