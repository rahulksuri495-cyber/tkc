Event Planners in Delhi

Quick answer

TKC Talent is a team of event planners in Delhi, based in CR Park since 2005. One named planner takes your event from brief to settlement: budget, venue, timeline, vendors, permits, artists and show-day running. Corporate events can be planned in 14 days; weddings and large shows need 4–6 months.

  • Planner model: One accountable planner per event
  • Fastest turnaround: 14 days (corporate)
  • Agency fee: 8–15% of budget or fixed fee
  • Smallest projects: From ₹3 lakh
  • Permits handled: Delhi Police, Fire, MCD/NDMC, DPCC
  • Office: 600, CR Park, New Delhi

Permits for events in Delhi

Missing approvals are one of the most common reasons Delhi events get delayed. Our planners file and track them for you:

ApprovalUsually needed for
Delhi PolicePublic gatherings, traffic and late-night events
Delhi Fire ServiceTemporary structures, large indoor gatherings
MCD / NDMCUse of public land, hoardings and temporary structures
DPCCAmplified sound and generators
Venue approvalsLoad-in times, fire plans and insurance at hotels and halls

What an event planner does — and what you do

  • We do: budget plan, venue shortlist, vendor contracts, design, stage and sound, artist booking, permits, guest logistics, run-of-show and settlement.
  • You do: approve the brief and budget, choose from shortlisted options, share guest lists and sign off final designs.
  • On the day: your planner runs the show; you host.

Questions to ask any event planner in Delhi

  1. Who exactly will be my planner, and will they be there on the day?
  2. Which Delhi venues have you worked at in the last year?
  3. Which permits will my event need, and who files them?
  4. Is stage, sound and lighting your own or rented through someone else?
  5. Can I see an itemised quote with GST shown separately?

Frequently asked questions

How much do event planners in Delhi charge?

Most charge 8–15% of the event budget or a fixed project fee. TKC Talent's smallest corporate projects start at around ₹3 lakh in total budget.

How far in advance should I book an event planner in Delhi?

Four to eight weeks for most corporate events, and four to six months for weddings and concerts in the October–February season. A rush corporate event can be done in 14 days.

Do event planners in Delhi handle police and fire permissions?

Good planners do. TKC Talent files and tracks Delhi Police, Fire, MCD/NDMC and DPCC approvals where the event needs them.

Can I hire a planner only for the event day?

Yes, as day-of coordination, but most of the value is in planning: venue, contracts and permits. We recommend involving a planner from the brief.

Do you plan small events?

Yes. Boutique corporate events, private parties and intimate functions are planned by the same team.

How do I contact TKC Talent's planners?

Use the enquiry form or email hello@tkctalent.com with your date, guest count and budget.
