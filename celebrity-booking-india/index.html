What does it actually cost?

Celebrity fees vary based on popularity, demand and event type. Indicative ranges below — final cost depends on availability, location and scale.

  • Bollywood Celebrities₹2 Cr – ₹4 Cr+per appearance
  • Singers₹50 L – ₹14 Crper show
  • Comedians₹20 L – ₹50 Lper show
  • Influencers₹5 L – ₹50 Lper campaign

Additional costs

  • GST18% on appearance fee
  • TravelBusiness class, return
  • Stay5-star accommodation
  • ProductionTech, stage & rider
