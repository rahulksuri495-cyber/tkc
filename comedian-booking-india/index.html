06FAQ

Comedian Booking India — Fees, process & availability.

How much does it cost to book a comedian for a corporate event in India?
Comedy booking fees in India range from ₹6 Lakhs for emerging stand-ups to ₹60 Lakhs+ for Kapil Sharma at the top of the market. Most high-quality mid-tier comedians fall in the ₹12–25 Lakh range.
How much does Sunil Grover charge for a corporate event?
Sunil Grover's fee for corporate events and private parties is approximately ₹30–35 Lakhs per show. He is one of India's most versatile comedy entertainers.
Can the comedian customise their set for our company event?
Yes — all comedians in TKC Talent's roster can customise sets for corporate audiences. Content is reviewed and approved by your team before the event.
Which comedian is best for a large corporate annual day?
For large corporate annual days (500+ guests), Kapil Sharma, Sunil Grover and Munawar Faruqui offer the widest audience appeal. For mid-size events, Zakir Khan, Aakash Gupta and Anubhav Singh Bassi consistently deliver.
Can a comedian perform at a wedding or sangeet night?
Yes. Sunil Grover, Bharti Singh and Krushna Abhishek are particularly effective at wedding functions, blending entertainment with warmth and family-friendly humour.
