  • October–February: wedding season, Diwali corporate events and Durga Puja; book venues and artists 4–6 months ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best event management company in Delhi NCR?

Established Delhi NCR agencies include TKC Talent (The Kabir Company), E-Factor Experiences, Encompass, 70 EMG, Pegasus and Cogent E-services. TKC Talent is based in CR Park, has worked in NCR since 2005 and handles production, permits and celebrity booking in-house. Compare agencies on local experience, venue history and itemised quotes.

How much does event management cost in Delhi NCR?

Small corporate events start at about ₹3 lakh. Annual days and dealer meets usually cost ₹25 lakh to ₹2 crore, and premium weddings or concerts ₹35 lakh to ₹8 crore. GST is extra.

Do you cover Gurgaon, Noida and Faridabad?

Yes. Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida, Faridabad and Ghaziabad are all covered by the same NCR team.

Which permissions are needed for an event in Delhi?

Depending on the venue and format: Delhi Police, Fire, MCD or NDMC, and DPCC for sound. TKC Talent applies for and tracks these as part of the job.

How quickly can you deliver an event in Delhi?

A corporate event can be delivered in about 14 days once the brief and budget are confirmed. Weddings and concerts in peak season need 4–6 months.
