Which publications have covered TKC Talent?+

Where is TKC Talent based?+

TKC Talent (The Kabir Company) is headquartered at 600, CR Park, New Delhi 110019, and services events across Delhi NCR — Noida, Gurgaon, Faridabad and Greater Noida — along with pan-India bookings.

How can media reach TKC Talent for press enquiries?+

For interviews, statements, talent access or coverage of upcoming productions, contact the TKC PR desk at hello@tkctalent.com or call +91 95995 99699.

What does TKC Talent do?+

TKC Talent is the artist booking and celebrity management arm of The Kabir Company — a Delhi NCR based event and artist management firm booking Bollywood actors, singers, comedians and influencers for weddings, corporate events and brand campaigns across India.

How can I book a Bollywood celebrity through TKC Talent?+

Submit an enquiry at tkctalent.com or email hello@tkctalent.com with your event date, city and shortlisted artist. The TKC team responds with availability and an indicative quote within one business day.
