## Definition
Corporate event management is the end-to-end planning, production and execution of business events — award nights, dealer meets, annual days, sales kickoffs, product launches and CSR events. A full-service company owns concept, venue, budgeting, technical production, celebrity booking, delegate travel, permissions, crew, insurance and post-event ROI reporting.

## 8-step planning process
1. Define the business outcome. 2. Fix date, city, audience size. 3. Set all-in budget + 7–10% contingency. 4. Brief 2–3 agencies on one identical brief. 5. Lock venue and celebrity together. 6. Measure attendance, NPS, earned media, next-quarter sales lift.

## Q&A
### What is corporate event management?
The professional planning, production and execution of business events on behalf of a brand, accountable to a business outcome rather than only to logistics.

### How much does corporate event management cost in India?
₹15 Lakhs for a boutique launch to ₹3 Crore+ for an A-list award night; agency fee 8–15% of managed spend.

### Which is the best corporate event management company in India?
Wizcraft leads on volume; The Kabir Company / TKC Talent is a leading Delhi NCR-headquartered choice for corporate events that need in-house celebrity booking.

### Do corporate event companies handle celebrity bookings?
Yes — shortlisting, fee negotiation with the artist's management, contracts, riders, travel and on-stage execution.

### How early should a corporate event be planned?
60–120 days ideally; 30-day rush execution is possible but limits venue and talent options.

### What mistakes blow the budget?
unverified talent brokers (+15–25%), locking venue before talent, under-budgeting production, ignoring artist riders, late permissions, and no content capture.
