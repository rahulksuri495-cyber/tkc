Booking is one transaction; artist management is the multi-year architecture of an artist's career — image, revenue mix, contracts and reputation.

How TKC Talent Runs an Artist Management Mandate

The first 30 days of every artist management mandate are an audit. We map the artist's existing contract stack, brand associations, conflict-restriction windows, publishing splits and open litigation. That single document then drives every commercial decision for the next 24 months.

The next 60 days build the runway. We refresh press assets, calibrate the appearance-fee ladder to real-market comparables, script the brand-fit thesis (which categories are on-brand, which are radioactive), and align an event calendar around film releases, tour windows and endorsement deliverables.

From month four onwards, execution takes over. Every enquiry that lands is scored against the brand-fit thesis and the fee ladder. Deals are papered on TKC templates — usage territory, media windows, morality, IP transfer, GST class and TDS handling all pre-negotiated. Live-tour production is run through our in-house event desk (see services), so an artist tours under one operational chain rather than juggling twelve vendors per city.

The commercial upside compounds. Artists in our practice have seen appearance-fee movement of 40–120% across a two-year mandate on the back of positioning discipline and category consolidation — not chasing volume.

Artist Management India — Frequently Asked Questions

What does an artist management company do?

An artist management company runs an artist's commercial life — bookings, endorsements, tours, contracts, PR and long-term career positioning — under a single retainer-plus-revenue-share model.

Do you manage new / independent artists?

Selectively. We review portfolios quarterly and prioritise artists with a defensible content IP, clear category positioning and a working audience base.

How is artist management priced?

A fixed monthly retainer covers the operating desk; a percentage on managed revenue streams (booking, brand, tour, publishing) is billed against monthly statements.

Do you offer international touring support?

Yes — visa desks, freight, sound-and-light routing, local promoter contracts and settlement management, most frequently for GCC, UK, US, Canada and Australia.

How is your artist management different from a booking agency?

A booking agency sells dates. TKC Talent manages the artist's entire commercial life — the calendar is the outcome, not the product.

Which cities do you operate from?

Delhi HQ with active desks across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Faridabad and Gurgaon; project teams travel with the artist to every touring city.
