02 · About

About Sunil Grover — India's Most Versatile Comedy Entertainer

Sunil Grover — known globally as Sunil and Gutthi — is without question India’s most versatile comedy entertainer. With a career spanning 25+ years across TV, film and live comedy, and 500+ live shows, and a body of work that defines modern Indian character comedy, Sunil represents the absolute pinnacle of Indian celebrity for any event, brand or occasion.

Born on 3 August 1977 in Dabwali, Sirsa (Haryana), he trained at the National School of Drama before becoming a household name through Comedy Nights with Kapil and The Kapil Sharma Show as Gutthi and later Dr. Mashoor Gulati. He has gone on to lead Amazon’s Sunflower, with acclaimed roles in Pataakha, Tandav, Bharat, Gabbar is Back, Pathaan and Netflix’s United Kacche.

As a brand ambassador, Sunil Grover has represented top consumer brands including Pepsi, Domino’s, Asian Paints, ICICI, Vivo, Tata Sky and more. He is a consistent presence on India’s comedy A-list and has been one of the most-booked entertainers for corporate annual days in India.
