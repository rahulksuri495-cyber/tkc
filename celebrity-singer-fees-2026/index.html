Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Arijit Singh charge for a live concert in 2026?

Arijit Singh's 2026 event fee ranges from ₹4 Cr to ₹7 Cr for a single Indian metro appearance, depending on venue size, exclusivity window and international travel. See book Arijit Singh.

How much does Shreya Ghoshal charge for a concert?

Shreya Ghoshal's 2026 concert fees are ₹3–5 Cr for a full-band live performance and ₹1.5–2.5 Cr for a shorter corporate or wedding set. Full details on book Shreya Ghoshal.

How much does Sonu Nigam charge for a live concert?

Sonu Nigam's 2026 live concert fee is ₹2–4 Cr, with wedding and corporate sets often at the lower end of that band.

Which is the most expensive Indian singer to book in 2026?

A. R. Rahman is the most expensive Indian singer to book — ₹5–8 Cr for a concert-scale production. He accepts very limited private engagements.

Which Indian singer is cheapest for a wedding sangeet?

For a marquee-name sangeet on a tighter budget, Kanika Kapoor, Asees Kaur, Palak Muchhal and Kumar Sanu deliver strong crowd response at ₹25–70 L.

Are Indian singer fees negotiable?

The list price is a starting position. Multi-city tours, longer notice windows, off-season dates and category exclusivity typically unlock 10–25% commercial movement.

What are the extras on top of the singer's fee?

Travel and stay for the artist and band, ground transport, hospitality rider, backline and PA scale, and — where applicable — GST, TDS and social-media usage rights.

Do singer fees include a band and technical setup?

Fees quoted are usually artist-only. A full live band adds ₹30 L–1 Cr and technical scale (LED, PA, monitors) is quoted separately based on venue.
