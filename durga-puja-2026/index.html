Durga Puja 2026 at a glance

Durga Puja 2026 runs from Maha Shashti on Friday, 16 October 2026 to Vijaya Dashami on Tuesday, 20 October 2026, with Mahalaya on 10 October. In Delhi, the centre of the festival is Chittaranjan Park (CR Park), where the Co-operative Ground, B-Block, K-Block, Mela Ground and Navapalli pujas together draw 5 lakh-plus visitors across the five days. Brands can participate by booking a stall (from ₹35,000) or taking a sponsorship package (from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹45 lakh). To book, call or WhatsApp +91 95995 99699.

Durga Puja 2026 — Frequently Asked Questions

When is Durga Puja 2026 in Delhi and CR Park?

Durga Puja 2026 is celebrated from Maha Shashti on Friday, 16 October 2026 to Vijaya Dashami on Tuesday, 20 October 2026. Mahalaya falls on 10 October 2026. CR Park (Chittaranjan Park) pandals in South Delhi stay open from early morning aarti until well past midnight on Saptami, Ashtami and Navami.

How do I book a stall for Durga Puja 2026 at CR Park?

Call or WhatsApp TKC Talent (The Kabir Company) on +91 95995 99699 with your category, preferred stall size and budget. We confirm availability at CR Park and other Delhi NCR pandals, share the site map and rate card, block your space with a 50% advance and handle allotment, electricity, fabrication and passes.

How much does a Durga Puja stall in CR Park cost?

Indicative 2026 rates are ₹35,000–₹70,000 for a small handicraft kiosk, ₹85,000–₹2.5 lakh for a food and beverage stall, ₹1.2–3.5 lakh for an FMCG activation and ₹2.5–7 lakh for an automobile display zone for the full five-day period. Final pricing depends on pandal, location within the ground and stall frontage.

What sponsorship packages are available for Durga Puja 2026?

Packages run from Title Sponsor (₹18–45 lakh) with pandal naming rights, to Co-Powered, Associate, Cultural Night, Bhog Partner and Digital Partner tiers starting at ₹1.5 lakh. Every package is customised around your branding, sampling and lead-generation goals.

Why is CR Park the best location for Durga Puja brand activation in Delhi?

Chittaranjan Park is Delhi's Bengali cultural hub and hosts some of North India's most visited pujas — Co-operative Ground, B-Block, K-Block, Mela Ground and Navapalli. The pockets around Nehru Place, GK, Alaknanda and Kalkaji deliver a high-income, high-intent family audience with several lakh visitors across five days.

How much footfall can a brand expect at CR Park Durga Puja?

Across the major CR Park grounds and our partner pandals in Delhi NCR, cumulative footfall over Shashti to Dashami typically crosses 5 lakh visitors, with Ashtami and Navami evenings alone drawing 1 lakh-plus people per night.

Do you also organise cultural programmes and celebrity performances at the pandal?

Yes. TKC Talent books playback singers, bands, comedians, anchors and Bollywood artists for Durga Puja cultural nights, along with sound, light, LED, stage and production. Talent budgets typically start at ₹3 lakh and scale to ₹1 crore-plus for A-list artists.

What is included in a Durga Puja stall booking package?

A standard package includes fabricated stall or raw space, fascia branding, basic lighting, one power point, a table and two chairs, vendor passes, and access for the full five days. Extras such as chillers, additional load, custom fabrication, hostesses and audio announcements are quoted separately.
