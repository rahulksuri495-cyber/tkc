We plan these around Cyber City traffic, building security rules and hotel load-in windows, and can add a celebrity host or performer through our in-house booking team.

Planning tips for events in Gurgaon

  • Traffic: NH-48, Golf Course Road and Sohna Road jam between 6 and 9 pm on weekdays. Set guest arrival after 8 pm or arrange shuttles from metro stations.
  • Office buildings: Cyber City towers need security passes and fixed load-in slots. Apply a week ahead.
  • Monsoon: for July–August outdoor events, keep a covered or indoor backup, because low-lying roads can flood.
  • Weekend weddings: resorts on Sohna Road and in Manesar book out early in November–February.
  • Festivals: Durga Puja and Diwali weeks bring large crowds to the Sector 29 and 56 grounds. Plan parking and stall logistics early.
