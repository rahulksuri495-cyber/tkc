06FAQ

Celebrity DJ Booking — Fees, process & availability.

How much does it cost to book a celebrity DJ for a wedding in India?
Celebrity DJ fees for weddings range from ₹4 Lakhs for emerging DJs to ₹40 Lakhs+ for top-tier names like DJ Aqeel and DJ Chetas. Mid-tier DJs offer excellent value in the ₹8–20 Lakh range.
Which DJ is best for a sangeet after-party?
DJ Chetas, DJ NYK and DJ Aqeel are the most popular choices for sangeet after-parties — they excel at Bollywood-to-EDM transitions that keep all age groups on the dancefloor.
Does the DJ booking include sound and lighting equipment?
Yes. All TKC Talent DJ bookings include full sound, lighting and production management through our in-house technical team. No need to coordinate a separate production company.
Can I book a DJ for a destination wedding in Goa, Jaipur or Udaipur?
Yes. TKC Talent manages DJ bookings for destination weddings across Goa, Jaipur, Udaipur and other premium destination wedding venues.
Are international DJs available through TKC Talent?
Yes, on application. International DJs including globally touring artists are available for large-scale concerts and high-budget brand events.
