06FAQ

Singer Booking India — Fees, process & availability.

How much does it cost to book Arijit Singh for a wedding?
Arijit Singh's fee for weddings and sangeet nights ranges from ₹1.2 Crore to ₹5 Crore, depending on set duration, band size, location and event scale. He is India's most requested singer for luxury weddings.
Which singer should I book for a sangeet night?
For high-energy sangeet nights, Badshah, Guru Randhawa, Neha Kakkar and Diljit Dosanjh are consistently top performers. For romantic, emotional sangeet evenings, Arijit Singh, Jubin Nautiyal and B Praak deliver the most impact.
What is Badshah's booking fee for an event?
Badshah's performance fee ranges from ₹1–1.5 Crore for weddings and private events, and ₹2–4 Crore for large-scale concerts.
Does the singer booking include sound and production?
Yes. All TKC Talent singer bookings include full technical rider review, sound engineering coordination, stage setup management and a dedicated on-ground coordinator.
Can I book multiple singers for the same event?
Yes — TKC Talent regularly manages multi-artist singer bookings for large-scale weddings, concerts and festivals.
